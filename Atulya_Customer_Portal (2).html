FAQs

How do I pay an invoice? +
Go to Invoices → click "Pay" next to any outstanding invoice → choose your payment method and submit your UTR number.
How do I download my account statement? +
From the Ledger page, click "Download PDF" at the top right. You can filter by date range before downloading.
Can I modify an order after placing it? +
Order modifications can be requested only before dispatch. Please raise a support ticket or contact your salesman immediately.
How are credit limits applied? +
Your credit limit is set by the accounts team and reflected in your profile. Orders beyond the limit will be held until dues are cleared.
