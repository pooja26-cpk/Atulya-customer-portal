FAQs

How do I pay an invoice? +
You can pay via standard NEFT/RTGS to our bank accounts. For online payments, use the Pay button on the Invoices page.
How do I download my account statement? +
Go to the Ledger page and use the export feature to download a PDF or Excel statement of your account.
Can I modify an order after placing it? +
Orders can only be modified before they are processed. Please raise a support ticket or contact your sales representative immediately.
How are credit limits applied? +
Your credit limit is set by the accounts team and reflected in your profile. Orders beyond the limit will be held until dues are cleared.
